Key Directives:
1. **Three Distinct Experiences:** Family Member (reassurance & coordination), Older Adult (dignity, 3-icon simplicity, large touch targets, voice-first), Professional Caregiver (shift tasks, resident roster, handover).
2. **Design Tokens:** Strictly adhere to Sangam color palette (Deep Blue `#17324D`, Teal `#197278`, Warm Cream `#F7F4EE`, Soft Sage `#EAF4F4`, Ink `#172026`, Urgent Red `#B42318`).
3. **Accessibility:** Touch targets $\ge 44\text{px}$ (critical $\ge 56\text{px}$), Atkinson Hyperlegible / Humanist sans, audio confirmations on key senior actions.
4. **No Covert Surveillance:** Location only on active SOS/check-in; senior can inspect and revoke permissions at any time.
